# Darkroom privacy policy

_Last updated: 25 November 2026_
**In short:** Darkroom does not collect, send or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no servers of its own. Everything you make stays on your phone unless you choose to share it.

## Camera and microphone
Used only to take the photos and videos you shoot in the app. They are processed on your phone.

## Your photos, videos and creations
Your photos, videos, and anything you make in the Studio or Photo Room (strips, collages, scrapbook pages, cards, flip books and GIFs) are kept in the app's private storage on your phone. They are copied to your phone's photo library only when you save them, and they leave your phone only when you share them yourself through the system share sheet.

## Saved copies
To notice when you delete a saved print from your phone's photos (so the app can offer to save it again), Darkroom checks the file names in its own albums ("Darkroom Film", "Darkroom"). On iPhone this needs photo library access, which it asks for once. It only looks at its own albums and never uploads anything.

## Darkroom Pro
The one-time purchase is handled entirely by Google Play or the App Store. Darkroom only learns whether you own Pro; it never sees your payment details.

## Your name and notes
If you give your name in the welcome tour or under User Profile, or write captions and notes on prints, they are kept on your phone and only used inside the app (for example on the back of your prints).

## Crash reports
Crash reports are kept on your phone, in the app (Help > Crash Reports). They are never sent anywhere automatically.

## Contacting us
Contact us in Settings opens your own mail app with a message addressed to us. It includes the app version, your phone's system version and, if there was one in the last week, the latest crash report, so we can help without back-and-forth. You can read and edit all of it before sending, and nothing is sent unless you press send. We use your email only to reply to you.

## Ratings and links
"Rate Darkroom" shows Google Play's or the App Store's own rating box; Darkroom never sees your rating. Links to Instagram or this website open outside the app, where those services' own privacy policies apply.

## Battery and notifications
Battery level is read only to show it on the digital cameras' displays. Notifications tell you when film has finished developing; they are created on your phone and nothing is sent to a server.

## Deleting your data
Throwing away a print or deleting a file removes it from the app. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored. Copies you saved to your photo library or shared stay where you put them.

## Children
The app is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
