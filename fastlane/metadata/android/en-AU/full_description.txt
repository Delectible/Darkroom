Darkroom brings back a physicality to taking photos.

Pick a film stock and shoot. The raw film goes into the darkroom where you can watch it develop. Your photos are then pinned to a corkboard. Write notes on polaroid photos and share from the app.

Choose a retro digital camera, and load the files from the cameras SD card to your device. Experience photography like it used to be.

FEATURES:
- Advanced film simulation algorithm. Designed to look as close to real film as possible.
- Accurate retro digital cameras.
- Fun and physical interface, made to imitate the real experience of photography.
- Customise film intensity, camera colours, interface styles and more.

The free version gives you one roll of film and one battery charge for the digital cameras each day. Darkroom Pro is a one-time purchase that removes those limits, and gives you access to additional film stocks and digital cameras. No account needed, data is never collected. You will never be pushed to buy the pro version.
