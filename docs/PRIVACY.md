# Darkroom privacy policy

_Last updated: 6 October 2026_

Darkroom is a camera app. It does not collect, send or sell any personal
data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no internet access.

**Camera and microphone.** Used only to take the photos and videos you
shoot in the app. They are processed on your phone.

**Your photos and videos** stay in the app's private storage on your phone.
They are copied to your phone's gallery only when you tap Save (Film Mode) or
move them to C: (Digital Mode), and they leave your phone only when you share
them yourself through the system share sheet.

**Notifications** tell you when film has finished developing. They are
created on your phone; nothing is sent to a server.

**Deleting your data.** Throwing away a print or deleting a file removes it
from the app. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored. Copies you
saved to your gallery or shared stay where you put them.

**Children.** The app is not directed at children and collects nothing from
anyone.

**Contact.** Questions: open an issue at
https://github.com/Delectible/Darkroom/issues.
